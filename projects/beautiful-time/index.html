a very simple meditation timer. turn the dial, sit, and a bell rings when you’re done.

I wanted a simple timer for sitting and couldn’t find one that was just a timer vs. shoehorning lots of other features in.

there’s no account and it doesn’t collect anything.

it’s free. there’s an optional unlock for more bells, interval bells and themes.

it’s on android now, ios soon.
