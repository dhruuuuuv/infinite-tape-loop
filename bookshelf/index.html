Anti-Library#

What’s an anti-library?
I like thinking about an anti-library as “tsundoku” (積ん読) - the phenomenon of acquiring reading materials but letting them pile up in one’s home without reading them. The term is also used to refer to unread books on a bookshelf meant for reading later. (wikipedia)
